Conflict · brand

The gold atmosphere isn't live in Lovable — it's purple

The brief says the gold/amber moving laser field is already live in the Lovable app and should be kept. It isn't. I read ZayraBrandBackground.tsx: the streaks are purple rgba(167,85,247), violet and cyan, over a full-page cosmic indigo wash. There is no gold in it, and the indigo covers the whole page — which is the thing the containment rule forbids.

I built this app to the locked rules, not to Lovable: gold field, moving, on black; indigo contained to the Ask Zayra panel only. So the two apps now look different on purpose. Worth telling the Lovable session its background is out of compliance.
